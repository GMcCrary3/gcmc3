mobile wisps of mournfully sorrowed opaque
happy sky
swims gun metal blue

proud towers of residence gleaming

and i have already said too much

her yellow stripes dance green glass and browned brick
with mechanical birds float
fed on the hope of now
life bisects shears black and white
these windows to my world
eyes fixed on Her's
cloaked in mournful public privacy

dishes small
dishes big
fighting to collect and display
adolescence
mocking wren’s great dome
her drops of a rose decay
breaking monotony
such commercial success
built by waterbirds dipping into the central horizon
my adopted city sings technicolor
but truth told i know
she has adopted me

impersonal story
swaying into the carpet
this personal choreography
symphonically absorbed in solitude and vision

forever changing
conjuring a palette of her own
the whore passes the globe
and death birds circle in the sea above

feel nothing

feeling all

crisp brown autumn
off in the near distance
silver inverted waves coastal gather
south
you see,
most ladders climb nowhere
and my feet are cold

markedly distant
giraffes white and blue seek out more land
to consume
feeding a beast natural
confused by empires old in their new birth

acts of instinct
acts of self harm
acts all natural
acts impossible possible

bells chime

sirens let you know
they have the power to repel
through rescue and denial

not all sounds have pictures here
some go by without more than a ripple
seeing true times of self
i notice that the ice grows warm here

dripping into past
things seem more dirty
more tender
more aware
less now than tomorrow
will be

i play a mournful tune on my fingers
stirring happiness
stirring pride

metal lions
protect my centre
majestic and still
now
Her galleries of memory serving only white wine
not to stain the floors

fingers blackened in dirty dust
this little black mouse scurries into the track
all the while
a flying dream brought me here

no regrets
only trouble ahead
i don’t push myself enough
never hold the past we are told
that is the way to move into the early afternoon chill
with snaking carriages of people moving effortlessly on my periphery

a real bird crosses our vista

i can feel the warmth of the sun
even though he has already passed over
me
you
us

untold history
here i live
this part of the city
holding more stories than big squares
surrounded by pizza hut

slow bus journey around the squares
slowly learning lust is but learned
down streets of collected carnal knowledge
past my own protective square
past home to great leaders passed

bells chime
haunting visions of the future gone
becomes its own hotel for a middle class elite
under brings under
tracks with different ways to go
all leading to more light

one mile past the real world
we dip into the untold

this nowhere i live
no great library to drink tea i

my adopted life
my adopted tale

innocence can still exist if you remember to stop and smell
Her oasis in the middle of streets
all blooming in emotions of now

childhood lost
but one minute ago
wandering through a jungle of looking
blindly marching into the abyss before me
frightened by old mendacities of promises long ago
mistakenly unaware this is the exploration of the new
forced
coming of age
familiar tan reflection gleaming in front of me
clouds darken to move the moment
reliably east to west
at least this time

my eye scans for something
more brave and
more clever
i smile
my own symphony singing
suddenly brought on by the smell of Her

an electric pulse buried deep in still cold may
morning air
the stirring of jet lagged tummy shakes to the core

amazing

big

so far away

trains constant moves past green walls of yellow carpets
these little tsunamis of self doubt
crash on my brain

my first glance
a large metal box
blinking in the glare
i stare out into the journey before me

not knowing of pain
not knowing of tears
not knowing of absolute joy

true love
waiting around the corner
true love
waiting around each corner
even down in a basement fixing bikes

clouds move on now,
but they now hover over me
giving new light to see through
new self to live
fresh ability discovered
to see
to open my eyes
to painfully recognise
myself
yet once again
then only do clouds pass
my happiness fused tight as a shadow
following always
detaching only in the dark
to protect and keep me warm
from a darkness eternal and unrelenting

slivers of spires in sight
Her minor churches
dancing in stillness
hidden by progress perpetual
amidst the chaotic movement of time
these solitary guards standing sentry

it is windy today
you can see it in the blue

odd when you make a small fuss over discovery
but we all need to enjoy
such things for the first time

learning unending
even in your dreams
only they can save you
leading us the place we are now

forever in the dark
dancing in shadow
