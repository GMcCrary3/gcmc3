again
you know what i want
i call across the void
away from what a boy is supposed to do
normality still luxury
it was never meant to be like that
operas playing out
bitterly to type
a moment in time
never alike
a voice against now
unknowing who will be there
but there you are
pleasingly discontent
with the universal acceptance that keeps us here
leaving me to wither in denial is something
we never knew
bitter words
never burned
cul-de-sac of childish want
sung with the voice of dispossessed fact
unaware of the force of then
we revel in our plainness
choosing choice is what we have always done
frozen memories only a neighbourhood away
this is what growing up in the burning plains of tomorrow will bring you
choice in voice and of how we will go
smiles struck in paraffin moulded conversation
!47
frozen in melting pangs of tomorrow
disarmed
smashed
tonight and always
