toasty white sacrifices fall deftly in the effortless night breath and i realise
that things are moving much quicker nowadays. its like someone turned on
the music and left it on while doing the dishes. i question how the rhythmic
pedals of the piano are enough to seduce me in my time of need. and then i
realise, it just is. there are times i sit here and wonder what a nightingale
sounds like when she sings, but the i remember back into that ex-local
authority hovel i used to proudly call my home. back to a time where i lost
innocence and gained responsibility. the first of many falls, i can feel almost
happy in the knowledge that i tried. or at least i gave the impression of
trying.

you see, when it happened, when he went missing and i knew in my heart
that it was for good this time, i had been there before. many many times
before. but never all there, the time it sunk in with the bouncing of my
stomach into my head and oh how the ground began to tremble. bright
light, utter sensation. i could even hear the shuffling of my guardian angels
backpack as i ran towards the past hoping to find one last bastion of what
once was. it was not there. he was not there.

i knew then that to make it easy on myself i would have to retreat into the
recent and try to forget the past. all those carefree nights of riding lions and
wrapping the future in red bows with golden ends. big white house
appearing over the red rock horizon and little red cars without roofs
zooming around my mind. our last summer trickling away into the sweetest
smell of youth. i think i understand what happened. i think i know why.
even today i sit here and wonder if it was what was supposed to happen.
sealed in my climate controlled eco-pod smoking my mind i think of what i
have lost but am reminded of what i have just found. when will i stop
wanting to win all the time? i try and try to put this fire out and for the most
part i am successful. i know that the promise of now is more than the sum of
yesterday exponentially squared. but the memory of that colour is one i
shall take forward with me into my new understanding. into my new fire
filled world. some say that spiritual rebirth is something that can only occur
when you want it to. i am proof that it is not.

sometimes i break my own rules. like now. returning to add to a memory
that i thought was complete, always tacitly aware that you can never be too
full up with the newness of what happened two lifetimes ago. i breathe in
my own knowledge swimming in faint delusions of ego soaked grandeur.
but nothing is ever the same these days. nothing can remain static when you
really want it to. can it?

broken paragraphs are a new thing to look into, as change is constant and
always something that should be sung by voices on high, including my
own. all of the dust settling from when i took letters from myself out of the
drawer, choosing between my old sense of completion versus my true sense
of compartmentalisation. big words aren’t always worth a quarter anymore.
good handwriting is what brings us here, now. and even now, i can see why
i have never split my thoughts. its just not natural.

so that is the last one, at least for now. blue green feels nice, especially now
that things are similar yet discordant. slowly going up the scales of self, i
once again can concentrate on one staccato feeling. one staccato time. never
changing, always evolving has been my order at the bar. and hopefully
through the passing of the measures, i can find myself holding true to this
last bastion of college days found self when i sat up in my lofty bunk bed
wishing i was anywhere but here. never realising that that song would play
and play and play throughout the stanzas of my life. its like i am back in
that nice ladies house. you know the one, the one who taught me how to the
play the piano. the one whose name i can never remember. but she never let
me go out there on my own, never let me run up and down the strings like i
wanted to. is that why i don’t play anymore? i s that why i try to write my
music with words? i am never sure of much these days. like always
seriously hanging onto that last strand of sanity, scared of going over that
edge that i can see so vividly in times like this. but i don't think i am the
only one who sees it. i know i cant be the only one who feels it. hunting
through all of my tired old versions of self that still live with the voracity of
selfless youth. the last time i crossed over into the place was long long ago.
in the past. something i am determined to write about, maybe even one day
answering the questions of today with. sad thunderstorms have become
beautiful smells and pressure changes of now. it is the future i haven’t
thought about in a VERY long time. little windows onto what i think might
happened have been closed for years. i can not remember the last one, the
last time. maybe that means it is to long ago. i am not sure. but i am so
afraid that i can not get there anymore. i can see into a future sense of me, of
what i might like to happen if i make it there. but for some odd reason i
have officially crossed the line of realising you can never see what you can
become. i am brought back to now, where i sit in my quickly getting dark
room and wonder if it is time to turn on a light. or maybe just go smoke
another bowl in the bathroom and decide that when i DO get back in here
that i need to turn on a light. but i don’t want to be too stoned. at least i
remember to plug in my ipod. that could have been disastrous. i feel the
depth of this piece shrinking. and that is how i know, at least for now, i am
done with it.
