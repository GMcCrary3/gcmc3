there are things i can say only after i have written them.

i write to find out what i think. occasionally, i discover something i would rather not have known.

these poems come from lived experience, imagination, memory, and the unreliable territory between them.

sometimes a poem gives me an answer.

more often, it gives me a better question.

read these words in your own voice.

mine has already had its turn.
