heading: "the unfinished sentence"
lead: "a poem nobody gets to finish alone."
---
i will write the beginning.

you will decide what happens next.

twelve words at a time, strangers will carry a poem into places i cannot anticipate.

every twelfth accepted contribution, the poem pauses.

i return. i read what has happened in my absence. i add my own words.

sometimes i will follow.

sometimes i will resist.

then i hand it back.

there is no correct direction. there is no predetermined ending.

the sentence will remain unfinished for as long as there is something left to say.
