a word. a thought. a fragment you have carried longer than you intended.

you do not need to explain it. you do not need to make it beautiful.

something happens when a private thought enters a shared space.

other people encounter it. they bring their own meanings. a collection begins to form without anyone knowing what it will become.

this is that collection.

your contribution joins a growing work made by people who may never meet.

leave a trace.

it does not have to be profound.

it only has to be yours.
