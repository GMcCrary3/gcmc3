these paintings are not illustrations of an argument.

they are the argument, the interruption, and occasionally the refusal to answer.

i know what happened while i made them. i know what i carried into the room.

i do not know what you will see.

that difference interests me more than any explanation i could offer.

the work begins with me. its meaning does not have to end there.
