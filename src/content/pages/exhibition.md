i made it. you decide what it means.

that is not an abdication of authorship. it is an invitation to question how authorship works.

a painting exists before you encounter it. the experience of that painting does not.

every viewer arrives with a different life. no painting can control what those lives reveal.

look. consider. respond.

tell me what you see.

i might be surprised.
