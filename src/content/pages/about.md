i did not become an artist because i wanted something attractive to hang on a wall.

i make art because i need to.

i spent seventeen years in london. i have lived several lives inside this one, some by choice and others by circumstance. i have learned that survival does not automatically produce wisdom, but it does give you material.

my paintings explore what happens when color takes over from language. my poetry returns to memory, intimacy, loss, contradiction, and the strange business of continuing to live.

i am the author of *beyond? beyond: words for a captive audience* and *act iii*.

i am less interested in telling you what my work means than discovering what happens when you encounter it.

you bring a history i cannot know.

that history completes something i could never finish alone.
