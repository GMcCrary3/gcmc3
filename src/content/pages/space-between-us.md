what can two people make together when they know nothing about each other except their words?

one stranger begins.

another answers.

neither knows who waits on the other side of the page.

selected writers exchange stanzas, building something neither could have written alone.

at intervals, i enter as a third voice.

sometimes i will interrupt. sometimes i will follow their lead.

perhaps they will understand one another.

perhaps they will not.

both possibilities interest me.

this is not a competition.

nobody wins.

the work is what happens between us.
